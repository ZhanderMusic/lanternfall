## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Music** | Volume slider and mute. Every world and every menu theme has its own track. |
| **Sound effects** | Volume slider and mute for menu and lantern sounds. |
| **Screen layout** | **Auto**, **Portrait** or **Landscape**. Auto uses landscape when your screen is wider than it is tall. You can switch at any time, even mid-run. |
| **Menu theme** | Change your camp's look and music. |
| **Check for updates** | See if a newer version is out. |
| **Permanent storage** | Ask your browser to never clear your save (where supported). |
| **Online backup** | Back your progress up online, automatically. Optional. |
| **Save as file / Import from file** | Save your progress as a file, or load one. |
| **Redeem a code** | Enter a reward code. |
| **Reset all progress** | Start over completely. Tap twice to confirm. This can't be undone. |

Music and sound pause when you leave the app. The pause menu during a run also has quick Sound and Music toggles.

## Saves

- Your progress **saves automatically** on your device.
- **Runs save after every wave.** You can keep one campaign run, one Endless run and one event run at a time, and continue them from the Play page. If you quit mid-wave, you replay that wave.
- **Moving devices:** go to **Settings → Save as file**, send the file to the other device, then there tap **Import from file** and pick it.
- Keep an export code somewhere safe as a backup, especially if your browser clears site data.

## Installing the app

Lanternfall is a Progressive Web App: install it once and it runs full screen, works offline and updates itself.

| Device | How |
|---|---|
| **iPhone / iPad** (Safari) | Open the game, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. |
| **Android** (Chrome) | Open the game, then tap **Install app** (or menu ⋮ → **Add to Home screen**). |
| **Desktop** (Chrome / Edge) | Open the game and click the install icon in the address bar. |

The installed app follows your device's rotation, so turning your phone sideways switches to landscape (on Auto).

## Updates

The game checks for updates on its own. When one is ready you'll be asked to reload. **Major updates** play a short cinematic the first time you open them, and you can replay any of them from the **Updates** page.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| No sound or music | Tap anywhere first (browsers only allow audio after a tap), then check the sliders and mute buttons in Settings, and your device's silent switch. |
| The board is sideways when I don't want it | Settings → Screen layout → Portrait or Landscape. |
| I'm on an old version | Settings → Check for updates, then reload. On iPhone, close and reopen the app. |
| My progress is gone | Import your last export code. If you never exported, the save may have been cleared with your browser's site data. |
| Lost a run mid-wave | Continue from the Play page. You replay the wave you were on. |

## Online backup

Online backup is off until you turn it on. Nothing is sent before that.

- **Turn on backup** backs your progress up automatically after each wave and run. No account or email is needed.
- If this device and your online save are different, the game shows both (level, embers, best wave, time) and you choose which to keep.
- **Sign out** stops backing up and leaves your progress on this device. **Delete my cloud data** removes your online save and sign-in for good.
- Your progress always saves on the device first, so the game works offline. Backup catches up when you are online again.
- Redeem codes are checked online, so redeeming needs an internet connection.
