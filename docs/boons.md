# Boons

[← Docs home](README.md) · [Play Lanternfall](https://zhandermusic.github.io/lanternfall/)

Boons are run-long bonuses you pick from a hand of cards. There are **53** in all: 17 Common, 22 Rare, 6 Legendary, 5 Mythic, 3 Exotic.

## Boon XP

You earn boon XP for:

| Action | Boon XP |
|---|---:|
| Snuffing a foe | 1 |
| Snuffing a split-off or summoned foe | 0.3 |
| Snuffing a mini-boss | 6 |
| Snuffing a boss | 15 |
| Placing a lantern | 2 |
| Buying an upgrade tier | 3 + the new tier (4 to 8) |
| Ascending a lantern | 15 |

Each level needs more XP than the last:

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 5 | 10 | 15 | 20 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| XP needed | 45 | 58 | 76 | 129 | 477 | 1772 | 6579 |

The Boon scholar camp upgrade and the Study boon make you earn boon XP faster.

## The pick

- When the bar fills you get a boon pick. If a wave is running, it waits until the wave ends. If several levels fill at once, you get all the picks one after another.
- A hand has **3 cards**. The camp upgrades **Wider fan** and **Widest fan** add a 4th and 5th, and **Trophy hand** adds one more on every 5th wave.
- **Skip** takes some gold instead (25, more with Patience).
- **Reroll** draws a new hand, once you've bought Second thoughts.
- **Head start** (camp upgrade) gives you a free pick before the first wave.
- **Morning gift** (camp upgrade) starts each run with free boons.

## Rarity and odds

| Rarity | Boons | Chance per card |
|---|---:|---:|
| Common | 17 | 59% |
| Rare | 22 | 35% |
| Legendary | 6 | 4.2% |
| Mythic | 5 | 1.6% |
| Exotic | 3 | 0.3% |

These odds assume every lantern is unlocked and nothing is excluded. **Lucky draw** and **High roller** (camp upgrades) raise the chance of rarer cards. The Boons page in camp shows your current odds.

## What gets offered

- **Lantern boons** (marked with a lantern's name) only appear while that lantern is **on the board** in your current run.
- **Once per run** boons can only be picked once.
- **Surge** and **Moonfall** give free upgrades, so they never appear when none of your lanterns can be upgraded: not as your Head start pick, and not once every lantern on the board is maxed (or locked by mastery).
- Everything else can be picked again, and stacks.
