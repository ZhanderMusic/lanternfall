## Embers

Every run pays embers, **win or lose**:

- **Waves:** each cleared wave pays 3 embers, plus 1 more for every 4 waves (wave 4 pays 4, wave 8 pays 5 and so on). Waves past 40, if you keep going after winning, pay ×0.25, and so do bosses snuffed and XP and mastery earned past wave 40.
- **Bosses:** 5 embers for every boss snuffed (Trophy hunter adds more).
- **Victory:** 30 embers for winning a campaign.

The total is multiplied by:

| Bonus | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| Depth | +25% per depth |
| Difficulty | Easy ×0.6, Normal ×1, Hard ×1.5, Nightmare ×2.25, Oblivion ×3.5 |
| Ember jar (camp upgrade) | +5% per level |
| Long night (camp upgrade) | +4% per level in Endless |
| Daring (camp upgrade) | +3% per level on Hard, Nightmare and Oblivion |

Spend embers on [camp upgrades](camp-upgrades.md), lantern unlocks and skins.

## XP and player level

Every run also earns **XP**: 4 per wave cleared (plus 1 more every 4 waves), 20 per boss and 150 for a win. XP gets **+20% per depth**, the same difficulty multiplier as embers, and the Night school, Long night and Daring camp upgrades.

XP raises your **player level** (level 2 needs 70 XP and each level after needs 10 more than the last, about 55,000 XP for all 100) and your **Season Pass tier** at the same time. Levels go up to **100**.

## Prestige

At **level 100**, open **Prestige** on the title screen to start the climb again. You can prestige up to **10 times**.

| You keep | Starts over |
|---|---|
| Moonstones | Level and XP (back to 1) |
| Both passes and everything claimed from them | Embers |
| Every skin and menu theme | Camp upgrades |
| Lanterns you unlocked, their mastery and mastery prestige | Worlds unlocked and beaten, best waves |
| Codes, lifetime stats and settings | Saved runs |

- **Prestige I** unlocks **Oblivion**, a difficulty above Nightmare: ×3.1 foe health, ×1.24 speed, 5 starting lives and ×3.5 embers and XP.
- Every prestige level adds **+20% embers and XP** from runs, so each climb goes faster (up to +200% at Prestige X).
- Every prestige opens **2 Prestige skins** in the **Prestige shop** (20 in all). See [Skins](#skins).
- Level-up moonstones and first-clear moonstones are paid again on every climb.
- Lanterns prestige separately: see [Mastery prestige](lanterns.md#mastery).

## Moonstones

Moonstones are the premium currency. You earn them from:

- **Level ups:** 10 per level, 25 on every 5th level.
- **First clears:** 20 the first time you win each world (Pathfinder adds more).
- **The Season Pass:** every 5th tier, plus bonus tiers.
- **The event pass:** small amounts in its filler tiers.
- **Redeem codes.**

Spend them in the **Shop** on skins, Season lanterns, ember packs and the premium pass.

## Season Pass

**Season 1: The Hollow Moon.** A 100-tier pass that tells the story of the hollow moon, filled by the same XP as your player level. It has a **free track** and a **premium track**, which costs **150 moonstones** and unlocks every premium tier you've already reached. Tier 1 costs 120 XP and each tier after costs 8 more, up to 912 for tier 100 (51,600 XP in all).

- Most tiers pay embers. Every 5th tier pays moonstones.
- The premium track has the three Season lanterns: **Moonwell** (tier 1), **Hollow Bell** (tier 50) and **Eclipse** (tier 100).
- If you already own a skin from the pass, you get 50 embers instead.

## Shop

- **Featured:** six items that change **every hour**, at **25% off**. They include shop-only skins you can't get anywhere else.
- **Premium skins:** Mythic, Celestial and Exotic skins for moonstones.
- **Season lanterns:** Moonwell, Hollow Bell and Eclipse for moonstones, if you haven't got them from the pass.
- **Ember packs:** 10 moonstones → 80 embers, 25 moonstones → 220 embers, 60 moonstones → 600 embers.
- **Event shop** (during events): an Event skin for every lantern (75 moonstones each), the event's menu theme (50), or the whole set as a bundle (450).

Higher rarities look louder: Legendaries get a golden halo, then rune rings, light rays, orbiting sparkles and color-cycling shots as you go up. Event skins add their own touches, like circling bats and a blood-moon glow. A lantern at **mastery 100** also gets a **Radiant** look. **Founder's gold** has its own double golden halo.

**Prestige skins** are the rarest: turning sigils, tilted orbits whose orbs pass in front of and behind the lantern, a floating crown, light rays and wings at the highest levels. They flare with a nova when you tap them or when they fire, and previews lean toward your pointer. Two open with each prestige level and cost **350 to 980 moonstones**.

## Redeem codes

Enter codes in **Settings → Redeem a code**. Codes can give moonstones, embers or exclusive skins. Each code works once per player: your save remembers it, and so does your device, so resetting or prestiging can't redeem it again.

A secret code unlocks the **Founders Edition**: Founder's gold for every lantern, the Season 1 premium pass (while the season runs) and a **Founders Edition** title under the logo. Hide or show the title in Settings.
