# Strategy guide

[← Docs home](README.md) · [Play Lanternfall](https://zhandermusic.github.io/lanternfall/)

Tips for getting deeper, from the first few depths to Nightmare.

## Placement

- **Cover bends.** The inside of a sharp bend lets one lantern hit the same foe for much longer. A spot where the trail loops back on itself is even better.
- **Build a kill zone.** Pick one stretch of trail and stack your lanterns around it. Put **Frost** or the **Hollow Bell** at the **start** of the zone so slowed foes spend longer in it.
- **Long range at the back.** Comet, Eclipse and Lance reach far, so they can sit behind your short-range lanterns.
- **Beacons in the middle.** A Beacon boosts the 8 tiles around it, so surround it with lanterns.
- **Watch the dashed circle** while dragging. It shows exactly what the lantern will reach.

## Building a team

A strong team covers four jobs:

| Job | Good picks |
|---|---|
| Single-target damage (bosses, tough foes) | Spark, Lance, Moonwell, Eclipse |
| Crowd damage (swarms, broods, splitters) | Cinder, Bolt, Comet |
| Control (slow or freeze foes) | Frost, Hollow Bell, Venom's Neurotoxin |
| Armor answer | Venom, Moonwell, Eclipse, armor-piercing upgrades |

Add a **Mint** early if you're confident: it pays more the longer the run lasts. Add a **Beacon** once you have a cluster of lanterns worth boosting.

## Economy

- **Mint first, or not at all.** A Mint placed on wave 2 pays back many times over. One placed on wave 30 barely matters.
- **Interest** (Mint's Bank path and the Compound camp upgrade) rewards keeping some gold banked between waves.
- **The halfway bonus** pays out mid-wave, so you can often afford an upgrade before the wave ends.
- **Don't oversell.** Selling only returns 60% by default.

## Upgrades and ascension

- **Tier 3–5 upgrades** are where lanterns turn on: multi-shot, crits, armor piercing, burns and stuns.
- **Ascend your carry.** Pick your best lantern and push it to ascension. Then build a second copy and merge for Ascended II.
- **Pick the ascension for the job.** For example, Spark's Overcharge is pure damage, Static field makes every other lantern hit harder, and Starburst handles crowds.

## Boons

- **Common boons stack.** Several copies of Fiercer flame or Quick hands add up fast.
- **Lantern boons** only show up when that lantern is on the board, so build your main lantern early to see its boons.
- **Surge and Moonfall** give free upgrades to every lantern you own, so they're best when you have many lanterns with upgrades left to buy.
- Camp upgrades like **Wider fan**, **Lucky draw** and **High roller** make every pick better.

## Foes to watch for

| Foe | Problem | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Beetles, Ember knights, Moon titans | Armor | Venom, Moonwell, Eclipse, armor piercing |
| Broods, Magmites, Jacks | Split into more foes | Area damage behind your main kill zone |
| Ice skaters, Ghosts, Moon titans | Can't be slowed | Raw damage instead of Frost |
| Sporemothers | Heal nearby foes | Long-range single target (Lance, Eclipse) |
| Voidlings, Ghosts, Bog lurkers | Can't be hit for a moment | Lots of lanterns along the trail, not just one spot |
| Sand wraiths | Dodge shots | Area damage, poison and burns can't miss |

See [Foes](foes.md) for every foe.

## Pushing deeper

- Each depth adds **+35% foe health** and **4% speed**, and brings a new foe. Expect to need more camp upgrades and more mastery before the next depth feels easy.
- **Easy is fine** while you build mastery and embers. It pays ×0.6, but a win pays far more than a loss.
- **Endless** is great for mastery and for testing how far a build can go.
- **Hard and Nightmare** pay ×1.5 and ×2.25 embers and XP once your camp is strong enough.
