## How upgrades work

- Every lantern has **three paths of five tiers**. A lantern can only use **two of its three paths**: once two have upgrades, the third is locked. Max both to ascend. Each ascension is **linked to a path** (the first ascension to path 1, and so on), so the paths you pick decide which ascensions you can choose. Each ascension type can be on the board once at a time. The third path unlocks at mastery 40.
- Every tier also gives a **core boost** on top of its listed effect: about +17% damage and +0.05 range.
- Damage and fire-rate boosts from tiers are softened a little as they stack, and extra targets from multi-shot upgrades deal half damage.
- **Tier prices** are a multiple of the lantern's build cost: ×1.3, ×1.95, ×3, ×4.7 and ×7.4 for tiers 1 to 5, and then 15% more on top of that. Camp upgrades like Thrift and Appraiser lower them.
- **Ascending** needs both paths at tier 5 and costs 6× the build cost. You pick one of the lantern's ascension types: the first unlocks at mastery 30, the second at 50 and the third at 70.
- **Merging** two ascended lanterns of the same type and level makes Ascended II, then Ascended III. Each merge gives ×1.75 damage, ×1.2 fire rate and ×1.12 range.
- **Selling** refunds 60% of everything spent on the lantern.

## Mastery

Every lantern has a permanent **mastery level from 1 to 100**. It goes up at the end of each run: **1 point per foe** the lantern snuffed and **3 points per wave cleared** for every lantern type you built (Studious adds more). Each level needs a little more than the last (12 + 3 × level points, about 16,000 for all 100 levels).

| Mastery | Unlocks |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Path 1, tiers 1–3 |
| 10 | Path 2 |
| 20 | Tier 4 |
| 30 | Tier 5 and ascension |
| 50 | 2nd ascension type |
| 70 | 3rd ascension type |
| 100 | Radiant look |
| 100 | Mastery prestige |

Each mastery level also adds **+0.5% damage**, and every 10 levels add **+2% fire rate**. Check a lantern's mastery road on the Lanterns page.

**Mastery prestige.** At mastery 100, prestige a lantern from its Mastery tab. It goes back to mastery 1 and gains a permanent **+2% boost** (damage, Mint gold or Beacon boost), up to **10 times** for +20%. Everything its mastery unlocked (path 2, tiers 4 and 5, every ascension and the Radiant look) stays unlocked, and account prestige never resets it.

## Spark

*Fast darts, one target.*

**Role:** Damage · **Cost:** 30 gold · **Get it:** Unlocked from the start

**Damage** 6 · **Fire rate** 2.2/s · **Range** 2.3 tiles

- **How it works:** Fires fast darts at the foe furthest along the trail. Cheap, quick to build and good at picking off small, fast foes.
- **Strong against:** Swarms, wisps, bats and anything fast and fragile.
- **Weak against:** Heavy armor: every dart loses damage to it.
- **Tip:** Great as your first lantern. Pair it with an armor breaker or Venom once beetles show up.

## Sapling

*Grows stronger every wave and boosts nearby lanterns.*

**Role:** Support · **Cost:** 60 gold · **Get it:** Lanterns page, 20 embers

Passive: doesn't attack. It replaces the old Mint, and every Mint skin carried over to it.

- **How it works:** Starts small and grows one stage every wave it stands on the board, up to stage 20. Each stage gives lanterns within 1 tile more damage and fire rate.
- **Strong against:** Long runs, where it becomes one of the strongest boosts.
- **Weak against:** It adds no damage on its own and starts weak.
- **Tip:** Build it early. It only grows while it is on the board.

**Roots:** grows faster and can grow higher.
**Bloom:** each stage gives more damage and fire rate.
**Canopy:** starts with stages already grown, adds range to nearby lanterns, and reaches 1 tile farther.

Ascensions: **Evergreen** (grows twice as fast, 10 stages higher), **Wild seed** (nearby lanterns gain crit chance per stage), **World tree** (reaches farther, grows higher, more damage per stage).
