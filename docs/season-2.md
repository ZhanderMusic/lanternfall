# Season 2: The Simmering Moon

Season 2 starts on November 1, 2026 and runs until January 31, 2027. It replaces the Season 1 pass and the Hollow Harvest event.

## The pass

- **150 tiers.** Tiers 1 to 100, then bonus tiers at 105, 110, 125 and 150.
- **Free and premium lanes.** The premium lane unlocks everything the free lane skips.
- **Three new lanterns:** Simmer (tier 1), Alembic (tier 50) and Philter (tier 100). See [Lanterns](lanterns.md#simmer).
- **Skins.** Ten Mythic and Exotic Season 2 skins, four hand-built Exotic looks (Cauldron Heart, Distilled Moon, Three Moons Rising and Brewmother's Crown), and skins for older lanterns.
- **Menu themes.** Simmering Moon, Bubbling Depths, Moonstir, Starbrew Web and Brewmother's Eclipse.
- **Your Season 1 pass.** When Season 2 starts, any Season 1 reward you reached but had not claimed is granted automatically, and the pass starts again from tier 1.
- **After the season,** its skins and lanterns join the Shop's featured rotation.

## The cauldron

During a run, foes drop four kinds of ingredient:

| Ingredient | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Ember Root** | +8% damage |
| **Moon Dew** | foes 8% slower |
| **Gloom Cap** | +10% gold from kills |
| **Starlight** | +0.2 range |

Mix one, two or three in the cauldron to brew a potion for the wave. Three of a kind or three different ingredients make a rarer potion that is stronger. Every combination has a name, and the **Recipe book** on the title screen keeps the ones you have found.

## The Far Hollow

Worlds 21 to 31 are new: The Simmering Hollow, the Brewmother's kitchen, then ten more worlds past the edge of the map. Each has its own foe, boss, twist and music. See [Worlds and modes](worlds.md) and [Foes](foes.md).

## Also in 2.0

- A **Performance** setting in Settings: Auto lightens effects on its own if your device struggles, Smooth always runs light, and Full effects draws everything.
- Every world and menu theme has its own music, and the harder difficulties sound eerier.
