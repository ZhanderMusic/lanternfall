# Camp upgrades

[← Docs home](README.md) · [Play Lanternfall](https://zhandermusic.github.io/lanternfall/)

Camp upgrades are permanent. Buy them with embers on the **Upgrades** page at camp. There are **67** across seven categories, and each level costs more than the last.

## Lantern unlocks

Unlock lanterns on the **Lanterns** page with embers.

| Lantern | Cost |
|---|---:|
| Frost | 8 embers |
| Lance | 12 embers |
| Venom | 15 embers |
| Mint | 20 embers |
| Bolt | 25 embers |
| Beacon | 30 embers |
| Comet | 40 embers |

The Moonwell, Hollow Bell and Eclipse come from the Season 1 Pass (tiers 1, 50 and 100 of the premium track) or the Shop.
