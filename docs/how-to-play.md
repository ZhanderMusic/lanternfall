# How to play

[← Docs home](README.md) · [Play Lanternfall](https://zhandermusic.github.io/lanternfall/)

Lanternfall is a tower defense roguelike. Foes walk a winding trail, you build lanterns beside it to snuff them out, and every run pays out embers you spend on permanent upgrades. Each run carves a brand-new random trail, so no two nights play the same.

## The goal

Foes walk the trail from one end of the board to the other. Every foe that gets through costs you lives (most cost 1, bigger foes cost more, a boss costs 8). **Survive 50 waves** to win a campaign run. If your lives hit zero, the run ends, but you still keep everything you earned.

## Your first run

1. On the home screen, tap **Play**.
2. Pick a world. You start with only the **Surface level** (Whisperwood). Win a run to unlock the next depth.
3. Pick a difficulty with the slider. Normal is the intended challenge.
4. Tap **Start**. You begin with **120 gold** (plus 10 for every depth) and **15 lives** on Normal.
5. Drag a **Spark** next to the trail, near a bend if you can.
6. Tap **Send wave**. Build more lanterns as gold comes in.

On your very first run a short **tutorial** walks you through this: hanging two Sparks (a pulsing tile shows a good spot), sending a wave, opening a lantern, buying an upgrade, building a Cinder and picking a boon. You can skip it at any time, and replay it from **How to play** or **Settings**.

## The run screen

- **The board** is a grid. The dirt trail runs through it; grass tiles are where you build. In portrait the trail runs top to bottom, in landscape it runs left to right.
- **The top bar** shows your gold, lives and the wave number, plus buttons for **Send wave**, **speed** (1×, 2×, 3×), **Auto** and the **pause menu**.
- **The tray** holds your lanterns, grouped by role: Damage, Area, Control and Support. Each one shows its price. Greyed-out lanterns cost more gold than you have.
- **Boons** opens the list of boons you've picked this run and shows your boon XP bar.

## Building lanterns

- **Drag a lantern** from the tray onto a grass tile. While dragging, the tile glows **green** if it fits or **red** if it doesn't, and a dashed circle shows the lantern's range. On a phone the lantern floats a little above your finger so you can see where it lands.
  - In portrait, drag **up** from the tray. In landscape on a phone, drag **sideways** from the lantern column.
- Or **tap a lantern** in the tray to pick it up, then **tap tiles** to place copies. Tap the lantern in the tray again (or press Esc) to put it down.
- You can't build on the trail, on decorations, or on top of another lantern.
- You can build at any time, even mid-wave.

**Range** is measured in tiles from the lantern's center. Most lanterns target the foe that is furthest along the trail.

## Upgrading, ascending and merging

**Tap a lantern on the board** to open its panel. From there you can:

- **Upgrade** either of its two paths. Each path has 5 tiers, and you can max **both** paths. Every tier also gives a small core boost on top of its own effect.
- Tap the **i** for a full guide to that lantern: how it works, what it's good and bad against, a tip, its current numbers and every upgrade.
- **Sell** it for 60% of everything you spent on it (camp upgrades and some boons raise this).

Some tiers are locked until that lantern reaches enough **mastery** (see [Lanterns](lanterns.md#mastery)): path 1 tiers 1–3 are open from the start, path 2 opens at mastery 10, tier 4 at 20 and tier 5 at 30.

**Ascending.** Once both paths are at tier 5, the lantern can **ascend**. Pick one of up to three ascension types (unlocked at mastery 30, 50 and 70). Ascending costs 3× the lantern's build price and gives it a powerful new ability.

**Merging.** Ascend a second copy of the same lantern, then tap the first one and press **Merge**. The two combine into an **Ascended II**. Two Ascended II lanterns of the same type merge into an **Ascended III**. Each merge gives ×1.75 damage, ×1.2 fire rate and ×1.12 range.

## Waves

- Press **Send wave** when you're ready. Nothing moves until you do, so take your time between waves.
- Foes come in **clumps** of eight with short pauses between clumps.
- **Halfway** through each wave you get half of the wave's clear bonus, and the rest when the wave is cleared.
- Every 5th wave brings the world's own **boss**: 1 until wave 15, 2 from wave 20 and 3 from wave 35. Each world's boss has its own tricks; see [World bosses](foes.md#world-bosses).
- **Speed:** tap the speed button to switch between 1×, 2× and 3×.
- **Auto** sends the next wave for you as soon as the last one is cleared.
- Each world introduces its own **new foe**, and you meet it in the first wave.

Foes get tougher every wave. For the exact numbers, see [How foes get tougher](enemy-scaling.md).

## Gold and lives

**Gold** comes from:
- every foe you snuff,
- the halfway and end-of-wave clear bonus (about 6 + 0.7 per wave),
- Mint lanterns, interest and some boons.

Gold resets every run. Spend it: gold sitting in the bank doesn't defend anything (unless you have a Mint's interest upgrades).

**Lives** start at 15 on Normal. Easy gives +5, Hard -3 and Nightmare -7. Camp upgrades and some boons add more.

## Boons

Every foe you snuff, every lantern you place and every upgrade you buy fills your **boon XP** bar. When it fills, you **level up and pick one boon** from a hand of cards. If a wave is running, the pick waits until it ends. Each level needs a bit more XP than the last.

Boons last for the rest of the run. They come in five rarities: **Common, Rare, Legendary, Mythic and Exotic**. Rarer boons are stronger but show up less often. Lantern-specific boons only appear while that lantern is on the board. See [Boons](boons.md) for all 53.

At the pick screen you can also **Skip** for a bit of gold, or **Reroll** the hand if you've bought the camp upgrade for it.

## Controls

**Touch**

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Place a lantern | Drag it from the tray, or tap it then tap tiles |
| Open a lantern's upgrades | Tap it on the board |

**Keyboard and mouse**

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Space | Send the next wave |
| 1–9 | Pick a lantern to place |
| U / I | Upgrade the selected lantern's path 1 / path 2 |
| M | Merge |
| F | Change speed |
| P | Pause |
| Esc or right-click | Cancel |

## Pausing, saving and quitting

Open the **pause menu** with the menu button (or P). From there you can resume, toggle sound and music, or **Save and quit**.

Runs save automatically after every wave. You can keep **one campaign run, one Endless run and one event run** saved at once. Continue them from the Play page. If you quit mid-wave, you replay that wave when you continue.

## After a run

Win or lose, the run pays out:

- **Embers** for waves cleared, bosses beaten and a win bonus, multiplied by depth and difficulty.
- **XP**, which raises your player level and your Pass tier.
- **Mastery** for every lantern you used.
- **Moonstones** for any level ups, and for clearing a world for the first time.

Winning a campaign on your deepest unlocked world opens the next depth. See [Progression and rewards](progression.md) for the formulas.

## The camp

The home screen is your camp. From there:

| Page | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Play** | Pick a world, mode and difficulty, or continue a saved run. |
| **Lanterns** | Unlock lanterns, see their mastery, and buy and equip skins. |
| **Upgrades** | Spend embers on permanent [camp upgrades](camp-upgrades.md). |
| **Pass** | The Season Pass and event passes. |
| **Shop** | Hourly featured skins, premium skins and lanterns, and ember packs. |
| **Boons** | Browse every boon in the game. |
| **Profile** | Player level, rank, stats and recent runs. |
| **Settings** | Sound, music, layout, menu theme, saves and codes. |
| **How to play** | A short in-game version of this page. |
| **Updates** | What changed in each version. |

## Beginner tips

- **Bends are gold.** A lantern at the inside of a bend covers the trail twice.
- **Start with Sparks**, then add a Cinder for groups once broods show up around wave 6.
- **Upgrade before you spread out.** A few upgraded lanterns usually beat many bare ones.
- **Beetles have armor.** Small hits barely scratch them. Use Venom (poison ignores armor), Spark's Armor piercer, Lance's Armor breaker, or big hitters like Comet.
- **Spend your embers** at camp after every run. Kindling (more starting gold), Whetstone (damage) and Hearthstones (lives) are great early picks.
- **Play the lanterns you like.** Mastery unlocks their later upgrades, so lanterns you use often get much stronger.
- Losing is part of the loop. Every run makes the next one stronger.
